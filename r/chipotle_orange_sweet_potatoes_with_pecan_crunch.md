Chipotle-Orange Sweet Potatoes With Pecan Crunch
------------------------------------------------

- 4 to 5 sweet potatoes (about 2-1/4 pounds), skin-on, cut into 2-inch wide wedges)
- 3 tablespoons canola oil
- Salt
- Black pepper
<!-- -->
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup panko bread crumbs
- 1/2 cup pecans, roughly chopped
- 1 tablespoon raw sesame seeds
- 2 teaspoons light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon plus a pinch finely chopped fresh rosemary
<!-- -->
- 3/4 cup sour cream
- 1 garlic clove, minced or crushed
- 1 tablespoon water
<!-- -->
- 2 canned chipotles in adobo, finely minced
- 3 tablespoons canola oil
- 1 orange, zested and juiced (1/2 teaspoon zest and 2 tablespoons juice)

Heat oven to 450 degrees F.

Add the sweet potatoes to a large sheet pan and toss together with 3 tablespoons canola oil, 1/2
teaspoon salt and plenty of ground pepper.  Roast for 30 to 35 minutes, turning them halfway
through, until tender and caramelized around the edges.

Meanwhile, melt the butter in a medium frying pan over medium heat.  Add the panko, pecans, sesame
seeds, brown sugar and 1/4 teaspoon of salt and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring frequently, until
golden and crisp.  Stir in the rosemary and set aside.

Stir together the sour cream, garlic, 1 tablespoon of water and 1/4 teaspoon of salt and set aside.

Whisk together the minced chipotles, oil, orange zest, orange juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt.  As soon
as the potatoes come out of the oven, drizzle over 3 tablespoons of the oil and give them a gentle
toss on the tray.

To serve, arrange the sweet potatoes on a serving platter and spoon over the sour cream, drizzle
over the remaining chipotle oil and finish with a couple of handfuls of the pecan crunch and a pinch
more of rosemary.
